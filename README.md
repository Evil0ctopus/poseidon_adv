## ⚡ About POSEIDON (Community Edition)

**POSEIDON** is a pocket-sized, keyboard-first wireless auditing and pentesting suite designed specifically for the **M5Stack Cardputer-Advance** (powered by the ESP32-S3 with a real QWERTY keyboard and 240x135 color display). 

This fork (`poseidon_adv`) builds upon the foundational GeneralDussDuss release, introducing enhanced stability, performance optimizations, and specialized features tailored for advanced field-testing and hardware evaluation.

* **170+ Recon & Attack Tools:** Comprehensive coverage across WiFi, Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), Sub-GHz, 2.4GHz, LoRa, Infrared, and LAN.
* **QWERTY-Driven Interface:** Letter-mnemonic menus and direct parameter typing eliminate thumb-stick scrolling.
* **Argus Autonomous Gotchi:** A reactive 96x96 mood sprite that hunts handshakes autonomously using an SD-persisted reinforcement-learning brain.
* **Multi-Theme Engine:** Switch between six distinct aesthetics (POSEIDON, Matrix, E-Ink, Synthwave, Phantom, Blood) complete with procedural ambient motion.

This Community Edition introduces several major upgrades and enhancements over the upstream repository:

## 🚀 Quick Start & Installation

Two fast, browser-based methods get you up and running with no local toolchain required:

### Method 1: Web Flasher (Recommended)
1. Plug your Cardputer-Advance into your PC via USB-C.
2. Open the official [Web Installer](https://generaldussduss.github.io/poseidon/install.html) in a WebSerial-compatible browser (Chrome, Edge, or Opera).
3. Click **Connect**, select your device's serial port, and click **Install**.

### Method 2: M5Burner
1. Open the desktop [M5Burner](https://m5burner.com/) application.
2. Search for **POSEIDON**, plug in your device, and hit **Burn**.
